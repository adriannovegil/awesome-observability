# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful place for
everyone who contributes, opens issues, reviews pull requests, or takes part in
project discussions.

We expect everyone in the project community to act with empathy, patience, and
professional care, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or any other personal
characteristic.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Credit other people's work when appropriate.
- Assume good intent, while still taking responsibility for impact.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation from
people whose behavior is harmful to the project or its community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any public space where someone is clearly
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior can be
reported by contacting the maintainer at adrian.novegil@gmail.com.

Reports will be reviewed with care and confidentiality. Maintainers will take
any action they consider appropriate for the circumstances, including warnings,
content removal, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
